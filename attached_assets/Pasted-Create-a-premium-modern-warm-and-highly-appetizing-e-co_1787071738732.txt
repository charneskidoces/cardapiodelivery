Create a premium, modern, warm, and highly appetizing e-commerce website for a cookie brand called "Cookies & Cookies".

The website should be inspired by the attached brand identity reference: a sophisticated vintage-inspired packaging design featuring deep burgundy/wine red, warm cream, chocolate brown, vertical cream-and-burgundy stripes, elegant serif typography, and a handmade bakery feeling.

The overall aesthetic should feel like a combination of a premium bakery, a nostalgic American cookie shop, and a contemporary Brazilian food brand. The design should be elegant but playful, cozy but sophisticated, and visually rich without feeling cluttered.

BRAND IDENTITY:

Primary color: deep burgundy / wine red
Secondary color: warm ivory / cream
Accent color: chocolate brown
Small accents of caramel / golden beige
Use vertical burgundy and cream stripes as a recurring visual element
Use elegant, bold serif typography for headings
Use a clean and highly readable sans-serif font for body text and buttons
Rounded corners should be subtle and sophisticated
Use soft shadows and warm lighting
Avoid overly minimal, cold, futuristic, or generic SaaS aesthetics
The visual language should feel tactile, handcrafted, delicious, nostalgic, and premium
